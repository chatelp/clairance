# SettingsPage

The settings open over the application: **one page at a time**, chosen in a grouped navigation (owner, 28/10, D86). From 768 px **a window over the application**, its pages in **a row of tabs**, each a glyph over its label (owner, 28/10: "a window in overlay rather than a narrow column in the middle", "a glyph per tab", "horizontal tabs on wide screens"); under 768 px the whole screen, the list of pages then a page.

**Pages, in three groups.** The navigation says whose setting it is:
- **This browser**: *Display* (labels' last transmission, smooth movement, frames per second, track points, ground aircraft at other airports, times in UTC or local; then "Diagnostics", folded: Live updates and the page's counters) and *Account* ("Signed in as", the role, "Sign out").
- **Station**: *Listening* (on air, reference airport, also follow, then "Official names (AIP)", which absorbs the former Regional data) and *Matching* (the presets **Standard** · Cautious · Experimental, then "Advanced", folded: the eleven rules, "Accept one digit off" and "First two digits" among them).
- **Administration**: *Access* (who may open Clairance, the accounts, "Live figures on the sign-in page"), *Usage* (who is here now, each account's week) and *Server* ("Log detail", the databases kept, their archive, then the reports, read only). The archive's folder is typed, or chosen among the server machine's folders with "Choose…" (`FolderPicker`, 28/10).

**Who sees what.** An admin sees every page. A **viewer** sees This browser and Station › Listening, **read only**: each value as text (`cl-field__value`) where the control would be, no button, no switch, and the page's lede ends "Set by an admin." Matching and Administration are not in a viewer's navigation. The page reads the role once (`/api/v1/auth/status`); the server stays the authority (403). One word for the role everywhere on screen: **Admin**, and **Viewer**.

**Markup.** `cl-page` (the veil, `color-scrim`) › `cl-page__inner` (the window) › `cl-page__nav` of `cl-page__group`s (`role="group" aria-labelledby` its `cl-label`, then links: a `cl-page__glyph`, the label in a `<span>`, a mark if any; `aria-current` on the page shown) and `cl-page__body`: `cl-page__head` ("Settings", a `StatusLight` "Saved", "Reload from the server" on a page of server settings only, close), then `cl-page__scroll` › `cl-page__pagehead` (`cl-page__eyebrow`, the page's group as a `cl-label`, none for About Clairance; `cl-page__pagetitle`, the page's name; `cl-page__lede`, one sentence on what the page covers) and its `cl-page__section`s, each an optional `<h3>` and `cl-field`s. On a computer the body's parts join the window's column (`display: contents`): the head, the tabs, the page, in that order, without moving the markup.
- **Reading width.** A section's rule runs the page's width; its fields keep 592 px (`.cl-page__section > :not(.cl-grid2)`).
- **Two columns** (`cl-grid2`): the accounts (Access), each account's week (Usage), the reports (Server), as `cl-card`s, in two columns when the page has 640 px (a container query on `cl-page__scroll`), one below. `cl-page__section--wide` is no longer needed: every section takes the page's width.
- **A page that needs a look** carries the status lights' shape on its tab, never a colour alone: `cl-page__mark is-warn` ▲ or `is-bad` ■, with a `title` saying why; on the glyph's upper right corner, like a badge, on a computer; before the chevron on a phone. The mark follows the page's own lights: ▲ for a light in `--warn` (the AIP list out of date, no archive so that the old databases are deleted, the archive disk missing), ■ for `--bad` (the last AIP update failed). A light in `--off` (the AIP list not made yet, by choice) gives **no** mark: off is not a fault.
- **Long lists in a card** show their last three lines, then a `Disclosure` "and 12 more this week" (the interruptions of Usage).
- **Actions** sit at the left, under the sentence they act on ("Add an account", "Refresh"), not pushed to the right of the reading width.

**The window** (from 768 px). The application stays in sight behind the veil (`color-scrim`, black at 70 %): the thread, the map, the column; the radio plays on.
- **Size: fixed**, whatever the page, so that changing page moves neither the tabs nor the close: `clamp(720px, 60vw, 880px)` wide, `clamp(520px, 84dvh, 800px)` high, centred, never closer than 24 px to the screen's edges. A page longer than the window scrolls inside it; the head and the tabs stay. At 1 440 × 900 the window is 864 × 756; at 1 920 × 1 080, 880 × 800.
- **Edge**: `radius-lg`, a `border-default` line, `shadow-overlay`. **No blur** behind: the map redraws at each frame of the aircraft's glide and a blur would be computed again at each one (not measured), and the motion seen through the veil says the station is live.
- **Head**, on `surface-panel`: "Settings" (title size), the `StatusLight`, "Reload from the server" on a page of server settings, close (`cl-btn--icon`, "Close (Esc)").
- **Tabs**, under the head, on `surface-panel`, a rule below: one per page, its glyph (20 px) over its label (caption), 64 px wide at least; the page shown in `surface-selected` and `accent-strong`. A rule keeps the groups apart; their names leave the row for the page's eyebrow and stay for a screen reader. About Clairance closes the row, alone. Measured 28/10: an admin's eight tabs take 624 px; the narrowest window (720 px) keeps 96 px spare, the widest (880 px) 256, room for three more tabs. Head and tabs take 109 px of the window's height.
- **Closing.** × and **Escape** close; Escape closes what is innermost first (a help bubble, a list open in a field), never what lies behind the window. **A click outside** closes only when the press and the release are both on the veil (a selection dragged out of a field does not close) and **only when nothing is being typed**: when a text field has the focus with a value not saved, or a form holds typed text (a new account, a new password), the click puts the focus back in that field and does nothing else.
- **Nothing typed is lost.** The window is hidden, never emptied. The field being typed is left before the window closes (`blur()`), so it saves as when left by Tab; if leaving it asks a question (a size kept that would delete databases), the window waits for the answer. A form that needs its button (Create, Set) stays as it was, hidden with the window, and is there again at the next opening, until the page reloads.
- **Focus.** At opening, on the tab of the page shown; Tab stays in the window (`aria-modal`, the application behind `inert`); at closing, back on the gear of the top bar.
- **Motion.** The veil and the window fade in `motion-duration-base`; nothing slides or grows; nothing under reduced motion.

**Glyphs**, one per page, from the interface glyphs: the 16 grid, 1.5 px strokes with round ends, `currentColor`, drawn at 20 px with the stroke kept at 1.5 px (`vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`). Display, a screen on its stand · Account, a person · Listening, headphones · Matching, two links · Access, a padlock · Usage, a week in bars · Server, two rack units · About Clairance, the information sign. The drawings: `GLYPH` in `tools/design/reglages.py`.

**Ledes.** Display: "How this browser draws the screen. Kept in this browser only." · Account: "Who is signed in on this browser." · Listening: "What the station listens to, and the official names it reads." · Matching: "How a transmission is tied to the aircraft that spoke." · Access: "Who may open Clairance, and with which account." · Usage: "What each account did these 7 days. Seen by admins only." · Server: "The server's log, its databases and their archive, then what it reports."

**Help.** The sentence under a field (`cl-field__help`) says **what** it does; the bubble on its label (`data-help`) says **why**, or what was measured. Never the same sentence twice. A value measured or decided for a place keeps its `cl-field__source` line.

**Renamed** (D86): "Measured" → **"Standard"** (the matching presets, and the setting of the procedure names: Surer · Standard · More); "Station console on the sign-in page" → **"Live figures on the sign-in page"**; "Log level" → **"Log detail"**; "Callsign matching" → **"Matching"**; "System" → **"Server"**.

**Phone** (under 768 px): the whole screen, no window, no veil; **the list, then a page**, on the model of "‹ Back to Traffic" and of a phone's own settings. The list is the phone's menu of pages: it opens first, so a page's mark is seen before a page is chosen. One markup, two states on `cl-page__inner`: `is-list` shows the head (title and close only) and the pages as 44 px rows, each its glyph (20 px, `text-secondary`), its label, its mark, a chevron, under the groups' names; `is-detail` shows the page, its head replaced by `cl-page__back` ("‹ Settings"). Two columns become one; a field whose value is a sentence (On air) takes `cl-field--text`.

**Loading.** Accounts, usage and the AIP list load at the first opening of their page; usage refreshes every 15 s only while its page is shown.

**Names from the AIP** (Q55, 15/10), in Listening › Official names (AIP) since 28/10 (D86):
- a field "Names from the AIP" with a `StatusLight` and its help, which says what the list is and what it is not, in words a listener reads: "The official names around the station, from the French eAIP: reporting points, control positions, procedures. They do not decide which aircraft is speaking: tried, a callsign whose airline is misheard sounds just like a procedure." (and, once the thread shows them, "They name the procedures and points heard in the thread."). No ticket number (Q55) and no "matcher" in the page: those are words of the project, not of the listener. The light in five states: ● "Up to date · AIRAC 2026-10-01"; ▲ "AIRAC 2026-10-29 in force; this list is of 2026-10-01"; – (`--off`) "Not made yet" (never "matcher" on screen: a word of the project, not of the listener); ◐ (`--switching`) "Updating from the SIA…"; ■ "Last update failed at 14:20Z: <why> (the list of 2026-10-01 stays in use)";
- `KeyValues`, read only: reporting points, control positions, procedures (counts), the area ("120 NM around the station") and the aerodromes ("LFPG · LFPO · LFPZ (the ones followed)");
- a `cl-row`: the button "Update from the SIA", `cl-btn--primary` when the list is out of date or absent, plain `cl-btn` when it is up to date, disabled while updating; beside it, `cl-plain` "Five public pages of the eAIP, about 9 MB, a few seconds.";
- the source line the SIA asks to cite, always shown when there is a list, `cl-plain`: "Source: SIA (DGAC), eAIP France, AIRAC cycle of 2026-10-01: ENR 4.4, ENR 2.1, AD 2 LFPG, LFPO, LFPZ. Made on 15/10 at 14:20Z. Next cycle: 2026-11-26.";
- a field with a `Switch`, "Update at each new AIRAC cycle", **off by default** (a request to an outside site: the server asks nothing of the SIA until the button is pressed or this is turned on), help "Every 28 days, when a new cycle comes into force, the server downloads the pages again."

**Naming the procedures in the thread** (Q55 usage 3, 16/10), in "Names from the AIP", under the source line:
- a `Switch` "Name the procedures heard in the thread", **on by default** (measured: at 0.95, 18 names on the owner's 77 cases, all right), help "A dashed chip beside the words gives the official name they probably were: "ELCOB 7G" for "elcope seven golf". The transcript is left as heard.";
- under it, a `SegmentedControl` of three settings, only measured ones, no free slider (a score between them would promise what nobody measured): **Surer** (1.10), **Standard** (0.95, the default), **More** (0.90); a `cl-plain` line says what the chosen one gave on the owner's listening: "18 names, all right" · "9 names, all right" · "19 names, 18 right". The field's help adds: "They name the procedures heard in the thread, from the sound of the words."

**What depends on where the station is** (owner, 16/10: the tool will serve other stations than Paris).
- **Every value that was measured or decided for a place says so**, on a `cl-field__source` line under its help, as the SIA's source line does: "Measured on Paris approaches (CDG, ORY, LBG), October 2026." (the matching presets), "Measured on the owner's listening of Paris approaches, 77 cases, October 2026." (the procedure names' setting), "Set for the Paris area: set your own for another station." (the frequency sectors, radii and ceilings), "Useful where French is spoken on the radio." (the French second reading). No decision number in the page.
- **The regional sources** (formerly a section "Regional data", in Listening › Official names (AIP) since 28/10): "Names from the AIP", with the naming of procedures and its setting (the matcher does not read it: it is a source for the thread, not a matching rule). Measured values stay with their function and carry their source line: a setting is looked for by what it does, not by where it was measured.
- **A source that does not cover the station says so, without controls**: the light in `--off` and the reason, "Not for this station: the French eAIP covers aerodromes in France (LF…); this station follows CYYZ." No button, no switch. When it covers part of the followed aerodromes, the source line ends with "Not read: EGLL, outside the French eAIP." (from the API's `coverage` and its list of aerodromes outside it).
