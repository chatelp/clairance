# Notices

Notices are the small messages that float: new exchanges below, and the browser's wait for a click before it plays sound.

**NewBelow** `cl-newbelow`: "↓ 3 new exchanges", at the bottom of the thread when the reader has scrolled up; a click scrolls down. It replaces per-frequency unread counters.

**Resume** `cl-resume`: the sign (listening state) and "Click anywhere to resume listening", centred at the bottom of the sky, until the first click or key.

**Notice** `cl-notice` (owner, 28/10): what the page says on its own, without being asked and without blocking. A card top right, under the top bar, over the context column (never over the map nor the thread); on a phone, above the tab bar, full width. `role="status"`, `aria-live="polite"`: it never takes the focus. One notice at a time; none while the connection banner shows (it says more urgent things), nor over the settings page or the sign-in (it waits for them to close). Markup: `cl-notice__head` (an icon, `cl-notice__title`, an optional `cl-notice__when`), `cl-notice__text` or `cl-notice__list`, `cl-notice__actions`.

- **A new version to load**: the page is older than the server (the server announces its version; the page knows its own). Icon ↻. Title "Clairance has been updated"; text "Reload this page to use the new version.", and while a frequency plays: "You are listening to 124.350: the sound stops for a moment, and a click starts it again." Actions: **Reload** (`cl-btn--primary`), **Later** (`cl-btn--quiet`). The page never reloads by itself: a sound may be on, a line being read. "Later" closes the card for this page; a button stays in the top bar, before the bell, until the page reloads: ↻ "New version" (help: "Clairance has been updated. Reload this page to use the new version."); on a phone, its icon alone, 44 px. The card comes back only for a later version.
- **What's new**: at the first showing of a new version, once per account and version. The sign as icon, title "What's new", the version's date in `cl-notice__when` ("28 October"); two to four lines in `cl-notice__list`, the newest version first when several were missed, then **OK** and the link "All the news" (Settings › About Clairance). Not shown on an account's very first visit (nothing is new to it), nor for a version without a line for the listener. Esc or OK closes it, for good.
- **Viewer and admin** see the same notices: they are about what the listener sees. The page's version and the server's are in Settings › **About Clairance**, below the groups of the navigation, for everyone (the commit beside the date for an admin only), then "What's new", every version's lines, newest first.

**Writing a line of What's new.** One sentence, at most 90 characters, in the present tense, about what the listener sees or can now do: "The settings are now in pages, grouped by who they concern." · "The sound starts within a second, and plays on an iPhone." · "After a cut, the page reconnects on its own and says so." A fix is written as the new behaviour, never as the bug. No word of the inside: server, sidecar, API, WebSocket, cache, commit, a ticket or decision number. A change nobody sees on screen gets no line.
